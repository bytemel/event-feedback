Feedback before people leave the room

Put one QR code on the last slide. People answer in 30 seconds on their phones. You see the score live, before the chairs are stacked.

Try it

The demo survey is for a pretend Demo Day. Answer it on your phone, then open the results and find your answer at the top.

Make your own

No sign-up. You get a QR code, a share link and a private results link.

What people answer

  1. How likely are you to recommend this event to a friend? 0 to 10.
  2. What worked? (optional)
  3. What should we change? (optional)
  4. Your extra question, if you set one.
  5. Would you come again? Yes, maybe, no.

The score is the Net Promoter Score: the share who gave 9 or 10, minus the share who gave 0 to 6. Answers are anonymous: no name, no email.
